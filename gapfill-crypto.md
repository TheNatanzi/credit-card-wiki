**Key structural traps for MS / gold-buying:**
- **Costco warehouse = MCC 5300 "warehouse club," NOT MCC 5411 "grocery"** — grocery-category bonuses don't apply in-warehouse. And **US Costco warehouses take Visa only**, so every **Mastercard/Amex** product below is unusable *in-warehouse* regardless of rewards.
- Almost all crypto cards pay a **flat rate everywhere** (no grocery/warehouse category), so "Costco counts?" mostly reduces to "is it Visa?" (Visa cards earn their flat base at Costco).
- Most are **debit/prepaid** — no credit-card bill to pay by debit (the Taekus-stack lever) → `N/A - debit/prepaid`.
- **Reward-currency risk:** CRO, WXT, PLU, NEXO, XRP, GNO, SCR float in $ value; the headline % is denominated in that token, not dollars. BTC-back and USDC-back cards are cleaner to value.

## Notes / open items

- **Crypto.com 2026 restructure (important):** an early-2026 "Level Up" overhaul cut non-staker rewards to **0%** and added subscription alternatives to staking ($4.99/mo unlocks Ruby-equivalent, $29.99/mo unlocks Royal-equivalent). Detailed 2026 sources (spendnode + sweepbase, which agree) give the current tier rates as **0 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5%** with monthly caps of $25 (Ruby) and $75 (Royal/Jade) and no cap on the top two tiers — a cut from the legacy **1 / 2 / 3 / 5 / 8%**. Stake sizes (≈5k / 50k / 500k / 5M CRO) are unchanged; their USD equivalents float with CRO price. **Lockup conflict:** newer detailed sources say **12 months**; some say 180 days — flagged, not guessed. An invite-only **Prime** tier (~$1M CRO → 8%) also exists. A separate US **Crypto.com Visa Signature *credit* card** (1.5–6% by tier) is distinct from these prepaid tiers.
- **US-available in 2026:** Coinbase Card, Coinbase One (Amex), Crypto.com prepaid tiers, Gemini, Venmo, Fold (debit + credit), Cash App/Afterpay, Robinhood Gold, Ledger CL, MetaMask (49 states), Kraken/Krak, CoinZoom, Uphold (Elite/Essential), ether.fi (~30 states). **Not US:** Nexo, Wirex, Plutus, Bybit, Bitget, Club Swan, Bleap, Gnosis Pay (US "planned"), MoonAgents (US "coming"). **Defunct / no card:** Upgrade Bitcoin (closed to new apps), BlockFi (bankrupt), Binance.US (no card), BitPay (paused), Juno (crypto closed), Strike & Unchained/Choice (never a card), hi (unknown).
