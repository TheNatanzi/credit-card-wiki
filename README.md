# Credit Card Master Wiki

287 US credit and debit cards scored by what they really pay at **Costco warehouse** (MCC 5300) over a full month, with caps, tiers, reward ceilings and debit-card bill-pay stacking applied.

**Live page → https://thenatanzi.github.io/credit-card-wiki/**

Ranked by **Net%**, never by the headline rate:

`Net% = Costco% at $50k/month + debit-pay stack − annual fee`

- 🏬 in-store = Visa credit or PIN debit · 🌐 Costco.com only = Mastercard · ✖ = Amex and Discover
- Costco codes as **MCC 5300 warehouse club**, not 5411 grocery, so most grocery bonuses exclude it
- Crypto cards are ranked in the same list with their lockup dollars and monthly reward caps
- Deep-research accordion shows the catches behind every card above the cutoff

Source and build pipeline: private repo `TheNatanzi/credit-card-wiki`.
