# Barclays US card lineup — gap-fill research

Compiled 2026-09-15 from Barclays product pages and card reviews. Covers the current Barclays US co-brand + general card lineup plus cards that are closing/converting.

## Issuer-level facts (apply to every Barclays US card)
- **Debit-pay = NO.** Barclays US does **not** accept debit-card (or prepaid/gift-card) payments toward a card bill. You add a **bank account and pay by ACH** (online/app/phone), or mail a check. Confirmed issuer-wide via Barclays Payments FAQ. So every row's Debit-pay = **No (ACH/bank only)**. Source: https://cards.barclaycardus.com/banking/help-center/faqs/Payments/
- **Costco note:** Costco warehouses code as **MCC 5300 (warehouse club)**, which is *not* the grocery MCC (5411). No Barclays "grocery" bonus category includes Costco. Separately, **Costco accepts Visa only** — all Barclays co-brands here are **Mastercard except Breeze Easy (Visa)**, so only Breeze Easy can even swipe at Costco (and earns just its base 1X there, warehouse being no bonus).

## Notes
- **Debit-pay verdict:** No Barclays US card can be paid with a debit card — ACH/bank transfer (or mailed check) only. This is an issuer-level policy, uniform across the lineup.
- **FX pattern:** Barclays waives the FX fee on all its *travel* co-brands (JetBlue, Wyndham, Emirates, Frontier, Miles & More, Carnival, Capital Vacations, RCI, Breeze) and on Upromise and AARP Travel. It charges **3% FX** on the non-travel/retail co-brands (GM, Xbox, Barnes & Noble, Gap Inc. Encore family, AARP Essential). Barclays View's FX is a variable **0%–3%** assigned at approval.
- **AAdvantage Aviator → Citi mapping:** MileUp→Citi AAdvantage MileUp ($0); Blue→Citi/AAdvantage Gold ($50); Red→Citi/AAdvantage Platinum Select ($99); Silver→Citi/AAdvantage Globe ($350). Same credit line & anniversary date, new card numbers.
