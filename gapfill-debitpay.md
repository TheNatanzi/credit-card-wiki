**Date:** 2026-09-15
**Scope:** Filling the `NEEDS RESEARCH` gaps from `agent4-debit-pay.md` plus the requested credit-union additions, and re-verifying Chase and Wells Fargo.

**"Debit-pay?" = does the issuer accept a *debit card number* as a payment method that runs over the card network as a purchase on the paying debit card** (the reward-stack requirement). Paying from "the bank account your debit is linked to" is an **ACH pull** and does not count (earns nothing).

**Third-party note:** Nearly every issuer below is *also* payable by debit through **doxo** (doxo charges your debit as a purchase, then pays the biller by ACH/check, for a fee ~$3.99 flat or ~2.99% for card). Where the native channels are ACH-only, the doxo debit path is noted in the Channel column.

## Notes / caveats
- **Credit-union pattern:** Almost all CUs treat a credit-card payment as an *internal transfer* from a share/checking account or an *external ACH* pull — **not** a debit-card charge. **Alliant is the standout exception** with an explicit one-time debit-card payment option. **Golden 1, SchoolsFirst, RBFCU, BECU, SECU, PenFed, Navy Federal** are all ACH/transfer natively; their only debit route is the third-party **doxo** middleman (which does charge your debit as a purchase, for a fee).
- **AFT/MCC caveat still applies to every YES:** a credit-card payoff funded by debit is frequently coded as an Account Funding Transaction (MCC 6012/6051), which some rewards-debit programs (possibly Taekus) exclude. Verify against the specific program before relying on any YES.
- **PenFed** official payments page returned a timeout on direct fetch; conclusion is from PenFed's documented Make-a-Payment flow (PenFed/linked external bank accounts) plus doxo's biller listing. If a phone rep accepts a debit card directly, that would be an undocumented exception — not confirmed here.
