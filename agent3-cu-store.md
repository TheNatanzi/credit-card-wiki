# Credit Union & Store/Co-Brand Credit Cards — Data Table

Research date: 2026-09-15. Compiled for the Costco gold operation. Focus: grocery/warehouse-bonus value.

**KEY FINDING on Costco coding:** Costco codes as MCC 5300 "warehouse club," NOT MCC 5411 "grocery/supermarket." Amex and Chase both explicitly exclude warehouse clubs from grocery/supermarket bonus categories. The one big exception is the **Bank of America Customized Cash Rewards** card, which *by policy* includes wholesale clubs (Costco/Sam's/BJ's) in its 2% grocery-store-and-wholesale-club category (up to $2,500/qtr combined) — and Platinum Honors status pushes that to 3.5% (choice-category tier hits 5.25%). That is the single highest-value card in this list for running Costco spend.

**Flat-rate cards** (no category bonus) earn their base rate at Costco with no exclusion, so a 2%–2.5% flat card is the practical fallback at Costco (Alliant 2.5%, PenFed Power Cash 2%, Navy Fed cashRewards Plus 2%).

## Notes & caveats
- **Costco in-warehouse accepts Visa credit only** (MCC 5300). Mastercard/Amex co-brands (Sam's, BJ's, Kroger) work at Costco.com online but not at the physical Costco register.
- **Debit-pay verified Yes:** Costco/Citi (by phone), Sam's/Synchrony, BJ's/Capital One. Chase (Amazon) and all credit-union issuers = NEEDS RESEARCH — CU cards are typically paid via internal transfer/ACH; debit-card bill pay is rarely documented.
- **BofA Customized Cash is the one card that turns Costco into a bonus category** (2% base, up to 3.5% with Platinum Honors), capped at $2,500 combined bonus spend/quarter.
- **Best flat-rate cards for uncapped/high-cap Costco spend:** Alliant 2.5% ($10k/cycle cap), PenFed Power Cash 2% (no cap), Navy Fed cashRewards Plus 2%.
- Navy Federal and PenFed supermarket bonuses (More Rewards Amex 3x, Platinum 3x) almost certainly exclude warehouse clubs by MCC, but the specific issuer terms were not confirmed → flagged NEEDS RESEARCH.
