# Points & Miles Valuation Lookup Table (2026)

**Value column = The Points Guy (TPG) monthly valuation, dated September 1, 2026** (primary source).
**Range = low–high across TPG and a cross-check source** (primarily Upgraded Points, Sept 2026; plus Bankrate/NerdWallet/WalletHub for currencies TPG does not publish).

All figures are **cents per point/mile (¢/pt)**. 1.0¢ = $0.01 = plain cashback baseline.

> Methodology note: TPG and Upgraded Points valuations reflect *realistic redemption* value (a blend of achievable sweet-spot and everyday redemptions), **not** the theoretical maximum. Actual value depends heavily on how you redeem — transferable points hit their high end only via airline/hotel transfer partners, not the ~1.0–1.25¢ portal/cash floor.

### Notes & caveats
- **Alaska Mileage Plan is now "Atmos Rewards"** as of 2026 (post-Hawaiian merger rebrand). Both names refer to the same currency.
- **U.S. Bank Altitude / FlexPerks and Bank of America are NOT in TPG's monthly set** — their figures come from Bankrate/WalletHub/Upgraded Points and are fixed-value programs (1.0–1.5¢), not transfer-partner currencies.
- **Discover has no transfer partners** — its cashback is a hard 1.0¢.
- Values change monthly; re-pull TPG's monthly valuations page for the current month before relying on these.
- Where TPG and the cross-check disagree materially (Citi, BA Avios, Hyatt, Choice), the wider range is shown; treat the Value column (TPG) as the headline figure.
