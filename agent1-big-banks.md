# Big-Bank Credit Card Wiki — Agent 1

Research compiled 2026-09-15. Covers consumer AND business cards from Chase, American Express, Citi, Capital One, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, U.S. Bank, Discover, Barclays, and Synchrony.

Key cross-cutting notes:
- **Costco accepts Visa only.** Any Mastercard, Amex, or Discover card cannot be swiped at Costco at all — noted per row.
- **Costco codes as MCC 5300 "warehouse club," NOT MCC 5411 "grocery."** Almost every issuer's grocery/supermarket bonus therefore EXCLUDES Costco warehouse purchases (Costco gas is often a separate story).
- **Debit-pay** = paying the card's monthly bill with a debit card (not ACH). This is rarely documented; marked per issuer where a payment FAQ could be reached, otherwise NEEDS RESEARCH. Confirmed issuer-level policies: Capital One = No (ACH only), U.S. Bank = No (no debit/prepaid/gift), Wells Fargo = No (debit only in-branch, not online), Amex = No, Discover = Yes (debit card accepted, not another credit card).
