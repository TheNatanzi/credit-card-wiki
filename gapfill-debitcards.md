# US Rewards-Earning DEBIT & Prepaid Cards (NON-crypto)

Research date: 2026-09-15. Scope = debit cards and prepaid cards that pay rewards on spend. Crypto cards (Coinbase, Crypto.com, Fold, Nexo, Binance) are handled by a separate agent and excluded here.

**Two important structural notes for MS / Costco:**
- **Costco US warehouses accept Visa ONLY in-store** (plus the Costco Anywhere Visa). Discover, Mastercard, and Amex debit are **not accepted at the warehouse register**. Costco.com online is more permissive (Visa/Mastercard/Discover). So a "yes" in the Costco column requires BOTH a Visa network AND that the reward is flat/all-spend (Costco warehouse codes as MCC 5300 "warehouse club," NOT MCC 5411 "grocery," so grocery-category bonuses almost never trigger at Costco).
- Most "up to X%" debit programs (SoFi, Dave, Sesame, Revolut, Varo, Cash App, Chime Deals) are **card-linked merchant offers / rotating boosts**, not a rate on all spend. The genuinely rare and valuable cards are the flat all-spend earners: **Discover Cashback Debit, Axos CashBack Checking, Amex Rewards Checking, Venmo/PayPal 1% base**.

## Key takeaways for MS / "earn on everything"

1. **Flat all-spend earners (rare, valuable):**
   - **Discover Cashback Debit** — 1% on all debit spend, $3k/mo cap, NO direct-deposit requirement. Best "no strings" flat debit, but Discover acceptance (not at Costco warehouse, spotty at some merchants) limits reach.
   - **Axos CashBack Checking** — 1% on signature Visa spend, $2k/mo cap, needs $1,500 avg balance. **Visa = works at Costco warehouse** and broad MS acceptance. Must run signature, not PIN.
   - **Amex Rewards Checking** — 0.5 pt/$ but transferable MR points (higher ceiling); Amex acceptance limits.
   - **FutureCard** — flat 1% Visa on all spend + eco boosts; earns at Costco.

2. **Best category rates:** **Chime Prime** (5% on a chosen category, $1,500/mo, needs $3k/mo DD) and **PayPal Debit** (5% on a chosen category, $1,000/mo). Both Visa/Mastercard respectively; grocery selection is MCC 5411 and will NOT trigger at Costco warehouse (MCC 5300).

3. **Costco reality:** In-warehouse is **Visa-only**. Warehouse codes as **MCC 5300 (warehouse club)**, so grocery-category bonuses do not apply. Only a **flat all-spend Visa debit** (Axos, FutureCard, base tiers) earns at Costco warehouse. Discover, Mastercard, and Amex debit are not accepted at the Costco register.

4. **Card-linked / merchant-offer programs (not a rate on all spend, weak for MS):** Cash App Boosts, SoFi (except Smart Card), Dave, Sesame Cash, Revolut Shops, Varo Perks/Upside, BankAmeriDeals, Truist Deals, Current, Bank5 Connect. "Up to 15%" headlines refer to these.
