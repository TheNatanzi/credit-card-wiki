# Debit-Card Bill Payment by Issuer — Reward-Stack Research

**Question researched:** Can you pay each US credit card's monthly bill with a *debit card* (as opposed to only ACH / bank transfer)?

**Why it matters (the stack):** A rewards-earning debit rail (e.g. Taekus) only earns when the transaction runs over the **card network as a purchase**. If the issuer only accepts ACH / routing+account number, the debit earns nothing. The stack requires an issuer that accepts a *typed debit card number* as a payment method — that runs as a card-not-present purchase on the paying debit card, stacking debit rewards on top of the credit card's own cashback.

**Date:** 2026-09-15

## Key rule on the "posts as purchase vs ACH" column
- When an issuer lets you **enter a debit card number** to pay, the payment is processed as a **card-network transaction** on the paying debit card → earns debit rewards. ✅ for the stack.
- When the issuer only takes a **bank account/routing number** (even "the account your debit is linked to"), it is an **ACH pull** → earns nothing. ❌
- ⚠️ Caveat on all "Card purchase" rows: a credit-card *payoff* funded by debit is often coded by the processor as an **Account Funding Transaction (AFT) / MCC 6012 or 6051**. Some rewards-debit programs (incl. potentially Taekus) exclude AFT/MCC 6012 from earning. Confirm against the specific debit program's exclusions before relying on it — marked `NEEDS RESEARCH` in the notes where it applies.

## Bottom line for the stack
- **Cleanest native YES (remote, no fee):** **Capital on Tap** — one-time debit payment in-portal, no fee, no stated cap. Best candidate for the Taekus stack.
- **Other native YES:** **Citi** (phone only) and **Synchrony** (Pay-as-Guest, one same-day payment/account) — both take a debit card directly.
- **Branch-only / impractical:** Wells Fargo.
- **Hard NO (ACH/bank only):** Chase, Amex (US), Capital One, Bank of America, U.S. Bank, Discover, Barclays, SoFi, Brex, Ramp, BILL/Divvy, Venmo & PayPal (in-app).
- **NEEDS RESEARCH:** Navy Federal, PenFed (official channels); whether Venmo/PayPal Synchrony cards appear in Synchrony guest-pay.
- **Universal-ish fallback:** **doxo** accepts debit for almost any issuer but charges a fee and the debit leg may be AFT-coded.
- **Not a path:** **Plastiq** cannot pay credit card bills at all.
- ⚠️ For every YES, verify the debit leg isn't coded as an Account Funding Transaction (MCC 6012/6051) that the specific rewards-debit program excludes.
